Welcome to NetProof · 4 quick steps

1 · Choose the network to referee

Demo loads a sample network so you can try validation with zero setup. Live discovers this machine's actual LAN (you confirm ownership first). Agent shows a managed network that checked in from inside. Pick the mode in the top right — everything updates.

2 · Explore the map

Every device renders as a Packet–Tracer-style icon on the Network map; tap a device or one of its service chips to inspect it. The map and legend always show the network you selected — never stale sample data.

3 · Propose a change

In the Change Builder, describe it in plain words (“block ssh from user-pc to app-server”), pick an example, or build it field-by-field. Hover the bold glossary terms for plain-English definitions, then press Run validation.

4 · Read the verdict

The referee replays the change over a model of your network and judges it: pass, warn or block. It's always a dry run — nothing is ever pushed to a device. Flip between Findings / Zone reachability / Diff, and export the report for your audit trail.
